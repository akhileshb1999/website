• LG Electronics India is the market leader in home appliances and consumer electronics, but FY26 — its first full year listed, after an IPO subscribed 54 times — was weak: revenue grew 1.0% to ₹24,605cr and net profit fell 23.5%. The immediate analytical task is separating cycle from structure, because appliance demand in India is heavily weather-dependent, with air conditioning the swing category that largely determines whether a year is strong or flat.

• The margin structure is the pressure point.
