• Lenskart is Asia’s largest eyewear retailer, operating 3,300+ stores across 14 countries. It derives ~60% of revenue from India and rest from International markets. Eyewear is a very fragmented industry, dominated by unorganized local players (~10% organized share). Lenskart’s focus on vertical integration and owning the manufacturing process allows it to offer a superior product compared to mass-market alternatives. Additionally, its high-quality customer experience and modern store design help attract and retain customers.

• Lenksart is significantly more backward integrated vs. peers, allowing it to operate at ~70% GM, which is the highest amongst retail peers. With growing scale and further backward integration, we expect Lenskart to continuously improve its margins from here (FY26 EBITDAM: 20% -> FY29E: 25%), driven by operating leverage on marketing expense (~10% of revenue) and other fixed costs.

• We expect the compounding engine to be driven by both store expansion (3.3k stores worldwide in FY26 -> 5k+ stores in FY29) and robust same store sales growth in existing network. This is underpinned by overall industry growth (~12% industry growth) and organized players capturing market share, resulting in ~25% revenue growth over FY26-29E. Limited competition in the organized space (2nd biggest players is 1/6th the size of Lenskart in revenue terms), further enables Lenskart to cement its dominance and gain incremental market share from here on.  

• Key risk: 1) Rising competition 2) Execution challenges associated with rapid network expansion over the forecast period.
