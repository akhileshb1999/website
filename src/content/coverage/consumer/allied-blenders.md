• ABDL is executing a portfolio pivot in real time, from a legacy built on Officer's Choice mass whisky to a P&A-led business now at 57.3% of sales. The vehicle is ICONiQ White, which crossed 10mn cases in FY26 having launched only in 2022, making it the fastest-growing millionaire spirits brand globally for two consecutive years. Reaching that scale in four years in a category where brands are built over decades is unusual, and it is the single reason the mix has moved as fast as it has.

• Margin expansion is being delivered by mix rather than pricing, which is the only way it can happen in this industry: FY26 EBITDA grew 28.5% against revenue growth of 11.5%, lifting margin 163bps to 14.4%. That still sits below Radico at ~16.8% and USL at 18.4%, so the investment question is convergence — whether a P&A share that continues rising closes the gap, or whether the legacy mass base structurally caps it [FY29E EBITDA margin: __%; P&A share FY26: 57.3% -> FY29E: __%].

• The backward integration programme addresses the input the company does not control: ENA is the largest cost line for a mass-to-premium spirits maker and the one that has historically compressed margins across the sector. The KION stake and the newly commissioned distillery move part of that exposure in-house [share of ENA sourced internally FY26: __% -> FY29E: __%], which matters more for margin stability than for margin level.

• Key risk: 1) Concentration of the growth story in a single brand, where any slowdown in ICONiQ White removes the mix engine 2) ENA and grain inflation ahead of backward integration reaching meaningful scale.
