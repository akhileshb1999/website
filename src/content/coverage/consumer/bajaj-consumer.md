• Bajaj Consumer Care is effectively a single-brand business: Bajaj Almond Drops holds over 63% of the light hair oil category. Growth must come either from expanding light hair oil penetration itself or from establishing credibility in adjacent categories, and the latter has historically been the harder problem for this company.

• The growth question is whether the revival is cyclical or structural. ADHO returned to near double-digit volume growth in FY26 having regained share, but durability depends on whether that reflects genuine category expansion or a favourable base and input-cost tailwind. Absent category expansion or a second brand at scale, the algorithm is capped by light hair oil penetration.

• Key risk: 1) Category concentration — one brand, one category, no scaled second engine 2) The operating leverage reversing if volume growth normalises, given how much of FY26 earnings growth came from margin rather than volume.
