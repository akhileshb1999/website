• Titan is India's largest organised jewellery player, with ~8.5% share of a market where organised retail is still only ~37% (up from ~2% in CY00 and ~30% in CY19), projected to reach 43-47% by CY29. Jewellery is an essentially undifferentiated product sold at high ticket size and low purchase frequency, which makes trust the actual product — purity assurance, transparent making charges and buyback terms decide the purchase, not design. Tanishq's brand equity is the moat, and mandatory hallmarking has structurally advantaged organised players by removing the unorganised trade's core cost advantage of under-caratage. Jewellery is >90% of group revenue; watches and eyecare are not the investment case.

• Margin expansion here is a mix story, not an operating leverage story. Studded jewellery carries roughly double the gross margin of plain gold, making studded ratio the single most important margin variable [studded ratio FY26: __% -> FY29E: __%]. Plain gold growth, however fast, is largely margin-neutral pass-through of metal price. This is precisely why topline and earnings decouple at Titan, and why studded mix and making-charge realisation matter more than the revenue print [EBIT margin FY26: __% -> FY29E: __%].

• The growth algorithm has to be decomposed before it means anything: FY26 revenue grew 34%, but gold price contributed close to ₹20,000cr of that increase, so underlying volume growth is materially below the headline [FY26 volume growth ex-price: __%]. The durable drivers are formalisation-led share gain — organised moving from ~37% to 43-47% by CY29, compounding at 16-18% against ~5% for the category overall — and network expansion, now increasingly international (170 stores added in FY26, 128 outside India; 67% of Damas acquired, adding 123 diaspora-market stores) [revenue CAGR FY26-29E: __%].

• Key risk: 1) Gold price reversal exposing the volume-versus-price composition of recent growth 2) Aggressive regional expansion by Kalyan/Senco compressing making charges in an already price-transparent category.
