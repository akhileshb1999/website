• Dodla is an integrated dairy company based in Telangana. The company derives the majority of its revenue from the sale of Milk and dairy-based value added products (30% of revenue) in the branded consumer market. Procurement scale is genuine, with milk procurement up 13.4% yoy and sales volumes at a record 13.6 LLPD (+15% yoy), but scale in liquid milk buys throughput rather than margin.

• The margin consequence is direct: FY26 EBITDA margin of 7.5% sits well below Hatsun's ~12.0%. The story revolves around margins improving from here on the back of increase in VAP share and backward integration of milk procurement (helping in shielding against dairy downcycles). 

• Growth is currently volume and geography led rather than mix led — milk sales volumes rose 19.5% and a Maharashtra plant is planned, extending beyond the southern base. Geographic expansion adds scale but replicates the same low-margin liquid milk model unless the value-added portfolio scales alongside it.

• Key risk: 1) Procurement cost inflation 2) Geographic expansion diluting returns if it extends the liquid milk mix rather than the value-added one.
