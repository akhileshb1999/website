• Marico's core is a coconut-oil franchise where economics are set by copra rather than by execution: Parachute holds a dominant position in rigid packs, but the category delivers volume rather than pricing power, and the input is an agri commodity the company does not control. FY26 made this unusually visible — Parachute Rigids volumes declined 1% in March while revenue grew 29%, meaning reported growth in the core was entirely price-led. The franchise is durable; the growth in it is not.

• Margins are therefore a copra call, not an operating leverage call. Gross margin was down 360bps YoY even as it recovered 140bps sequentially on copra easing roughly 35% from peak, and the squeeze is quantified in the gap between FY26 revenue growth of 25.7% and PAT growth of 9.3%. • The growth algorithm is the shift away from the core: Foods crossed ₹1,000cr in FY26 growing 16%, value-added hair oils delivered 26% value growth, and the digital-first portfolio was extended through 4700BC and Cosmix. The question is whether these reach a scale that changes the consolidated margin profile before the next copra upcycle, since at current mix the core still determines the outcome [non-core share of revenue FY26: __% -> FY29E: __%; revenue CAGR: __%].
