• Trent's economics are unusual for value fashion: it earns an 18.5% EBITDA margin selling largely sub-₹1,000 price points through Zudio, closer to premium retail than to discount formats. The reason is structural — Trent designs and sells almost entirely own-label merchandise, capturing the full brand margin rather than sharing it with a third-party brand owner, and pairs that with high inventory turns. Gross margin of 43.9% in FY26 against 43.5% the prior year shows a stable structural position rather than a pricing cycle.

• The 197bps of margin expansion in FY26 came entirely below the gross line, since gross margin was essentially flat — meaning it was operating leverage on a rapidly scaling store base rather than better merchandising or pricing. That distinction matters for durability: leverage of this kind continues only while the store base compounds faster than the fixed cost base, and it reverses if store additions slow [FY29E EBITDA margin: __%].

• The central question is the shape of the growth curve rather than its current rate. FY26 added a net 198 Zudio and 52 Westside stores, taking the network to 1,286, with revenue up 18%. But with 963 Zudio stores already open, each subsequent cohort moves the total less, so growth must transition from store additions to same-store productivity. Emerging categories — beauty, innerwear and footwear at over 21% of Q4 revenue — are the mechanism for that transition, expanding basket within the existing footprint [SSSG FY26: __%; store additions FY27E-29E: __; revenue CAGR FY26-29E: __%].

• Key risk: 1) The store-addition engine decelerating before same-store productivity is large enough to replace it 2) Competitive intensity in value fashion, where Reliance, ABFRL, Shoppers Stop and regional chains are expanding into the same tier-2 catchments.
