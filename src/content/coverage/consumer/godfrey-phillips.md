• Godfrey Phillips is the number two player in Indian cigarettes with roughly 14% of the domestic market against ITC's ~73%. Being the challenger in a category with an advertising ban is normally a permanent disadvantage, which is why the Marlboro license matters disproportionately: it provides the one internationally established premium brand in India that its larger competitor cannot replicate.

• We expect volumes to recover sequentially from here as consumers adapt to the tax effect. With the stock down over 40%, we see limited downside and more room for positive surprises, this is borne out empirically by resilient volumes following past tax hikes.

• Key risk: 1) Taxation-driven migration to illicit trade, which at 26.1% of the market damages the legal industry regardless of share position 2) Dependence on the Marlboro licence, an asset the company does not own.
