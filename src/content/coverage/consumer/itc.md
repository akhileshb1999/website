ITC's efforts to diversify from its legacy cigarette business are commendable, however, the inherent low margin profile and deep penetration of those FMCG categories fails to excite investors anymore. 

The recent tax increase on cigarettes in India will create pressure on volumes in FY27, however, given the relative inelasticity of cigarettes, volumes have room to surprise positively. 

Despite the stock undergoing a major correction, one can argue for the stock to outperform now (given that valuations have been the lowest in a decade), I think that the effect of any positive surprise will be short lived and make the case for tactical investment and not long term investment given that these traditional FMCG players will derate as their growth slows vs. new age brands.
