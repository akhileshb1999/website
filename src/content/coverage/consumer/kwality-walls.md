• Kwality Wall's is a demerger special situation rather than a conventional consumer story: HUL's ice cream business, carrying Magnum and Cornetto, was separated into a standalone entity that listed in February 2026 at ₹29.80, a 26% discount to the discovered price of ₹40.20. The question being priced is whether a business that underperformed inside HUL performs better outside it, with dedicated management, its own distribution and no competing claim on corporate resources.

• The starting position is poor and the peer gap is the striking fact: EBITDA margin of 7.1% in FY25 compressed to breakeven in H1 FY26, against Vadilal at ~18% and Havmor at 17-18%. FY26 closed with revenue of ₹1,909cr and a loss of ₹335cr. Owning premium global brands has not translated into premium economics, which points to a cost and distribution problem rather than a brand problem [FY29E EBITDA margin: __%; path to breakeven: __].

Until the standalone route-to-market is established, growth rates measure the transition rather than the business, and the organised segment at 60-65% of the market gives a category tailwind the company has so far not converted [FY27E revenue on a clean base: __].
