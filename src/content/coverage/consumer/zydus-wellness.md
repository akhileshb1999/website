• Zydus Wellness owns category leadership in small, seasonal niches rather than large categories: Sugar Free at ~96% share, Glucon-D at 58.9%, Everyuth at 48.6% in scrubs and 75.5% in peel-off masks, Nycil at 33.2% in prickly heat powder. At those levels the binding constraint is category size, not competitive position — there is no meaningful share left to take, so growth has to come from expanding consumption occasions or creating adjacencies, as attempted with Glucon-D Recharge in performance hydration.

• The portfolio carries an unusual earnings characteristic: Glucon-D and Nycil are summer-dependent and Everyuth partly so, which makes weather a genuine variable in the earnings line rather than a rounding factor.
