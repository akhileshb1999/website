• Sky Gold is a business-to-business manufacturer supplying gold jewellery to organised retailers, which makes it a picks-and-shovels exposure to formalisation rather than a bet on any single retail brand. As Titan, Kalyan and regional chains expand their networks, they outsource an increasing share of manufacturing, and Sky Gold benefits from that shift regardless of which retailer gains share — a structurally different risk profile from anything downstream of it.

• The economics are those of contract manufacturing, not retail: EBITDA margin in the 6-7% range, with value added through design capability and manufacturing throughput rather than pricing power. Margin has been trending up (7.34% in Q3 FY26 against 6.3% in Q1), and the specific tailwind is the shift toward lightweight and 18KT jewellery as high gold prices push consumers toward lower-weight pieces — a mix requiring more intricate manufacturing that favours specialist capability [FY29E EBITDA margin: __%; lightweight/18KT share of volumes: __%].

• Key risk: 1) Working capital intensity scaling with growth, since gold inventory must be funded and a 77% revenue increase implies a commensurate funding requirement 2) Customer concentration, where losing a large retail partner removes volume that cannot be quickly replaced.
