• Eternal is India's largest Food Delivery and Quick Commerce (Q-com) platform. Its Q-com business contributes ~75% to overall revenue and the industry offers a huge opportunity set. Eternal continues to dominate (45% market share vs. 2nd player at 23%) the market by a wide margin, however, the competition remains high. 

• We expect competitive intensity in Q-com to stabilize, and believe Eternal's strategy of growing through supply build out, as evidenced by stronger store additions vs. peers, rather than aggressive discounting will help maintain its dominant position in the industry. Rising throughput and efficiency gains from a larger operating scale should drive faster than expected margin improvement and cement its position as the most profitable Q-com player.

• Food delivery is gradually progressing towards its steady-state margin target of 5-6% of NOV (vs. 5.6% in Q1FY27) and evolving into a stable, cash generating business that is expected to grow at 15-20% CAGR over the near term.

• Smaller segments such as Going Out, B2B Supply and other ventures are likely to remain in an investment phase before they scale up. Eternal's Rs.18,000+ cr cash balance gives it ample room to scale these businesses without raising additional funds in the near term.


• Key risk: 1) Quick commerce competitive intensity forcing continued discounting, deferring Blinkit's margin inflection 2) Food delivery growth decelerating, which would remove the funding source for the quick commerce build-out.
