• V-Mart occupies the same value fashion space as Zudio but from a structurally weaker position, and the gap is visible in the economics: gross margin of 32.1% against Trent's 43.9%, and EBITDA margin of 10.9% against 18.5%. The difference reflects merchandise mix and own-label depth rather than scale alone — V-Mart's tier-2 and tier-3 catchments favour affordability over aspiration, which caps realisation per unit even when footfall is strong.

• FY26 shows competitive pressure being masked by operating leverage: gross margin contracted 95bps to 32.1% while EBITDA margin expanded 221bps to 10.9%. Discounting at the merchandise level was more than offset by scale on fixed costs, which works while stores are being added at record pace but leaves the underlying pricing trend unaddressed [FY29E gross margin: __%; FY29E EBITDA margin: __%].
