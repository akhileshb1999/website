• Skincare is the fastest-growing FMCG category, expected to grow at ~12% CAGR over the next five years. It's tracking the same arc traditional FMCG categories went through in the 2010s: high growth paired with margin expansion, as scale kicks in and premiumisation takes hold.

• Honasa is well placed to ride this, with a strong brand portfolio (Mamaearth, The Derma Co, Bblunt, etc.) spanning multiple skincare sub-segments. There have been execution missteps in the past, but the company appears to be on a course-corrected path now, with sharper focus on profitability and channel discipline.

• Key risk: 1) A repeat of channel-related inventory correction, given how recently the distribution model was rebuilt 2) Low barriers to entry in digital-first beauty, where the same performance-marketing playbook that built Mamaearth is available to every new entrant.
