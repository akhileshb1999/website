•	Groww is India's largest stockbroker by active client base, with a market share of ~27.7% as of May 2026 ahead of Zerodha (15.2%) and Angel One (15.0%). This leadership rests on an app-first, mobile-native model with a structurally lower customer acquisition cost than legacy and bank-backed brokers, and the gap has continued to widen even as industry-wide active-client growth slowed through the early-2026 market correction.

•	Groww has evolved from a pure-play broker into a full-stack financial services platform, spanning equities, mutual funds/SIPs, Margin Trading Facility (MTF), and its own AMC franchise. MTF book scale-up is a key earnings driver, where the company holds just 1.7% market share today, with significant room for gains. On the broking side, retail F&O ADTO share stands at 17% and we expect this to expand by atleast 500bps over the next few years, driven by deeper monetization of its large existing user base.

•	India's capital-market participation remains structurally under-penetrated relative to its population and savings pool. SIP-led mutual fund flows continue to rise as household savings shift from physical to financial assets. As the largest and fastest-scaling platform riding this multi-decade financialization theme, Groww is well placed to keep compounding its client base, AUM, and cross-sell revenue, most of which remains largely unmonetized today.
