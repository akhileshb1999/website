• Shadowfax sits on the opposite side of the structural shift that threatens conventional parcel networks: it serves quick commerce and hyperlocal delivery alongside express parcel, using a gig fleet rather than owned sortation infrastructure. That makes it asset-light and fast to scale — 14,758 pin codes covered, more than 72 crore orders fulfilled in FY26 — but it also means the moat is thinner, since the barrier is fleet supply and software rather than physical infrastructure that took a decade to build.

• The growth is the outlier in the sector: FY26 revenue rose 66.4% to ₹4,202cr against Delhivery's 17.6% and Blue Dart's 7.4%, with Q4 revenue up 73.6%. Crucially this is not growth bought at the cost of economics — Q4 Ind AS EBITDA margin reached 5.7%, up 270bps year on year, and FY26 net profit was ₹112cr, so the model is scaling profitably rather than buying volume [FY29E EBITDA margin: __%; revenue mix by service line: __].
